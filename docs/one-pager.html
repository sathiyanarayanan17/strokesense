The problem

In a stroke the brain loses about 1.9 million neurons every minute. Clot-dissolving treatment is mainly given within ~4.5 hours of onset, but many patients reach hospital too late because family members don't recognise the signs or wait to see if they pass. The BE-FAST checklist exists, but a frightened bystander cannot apply it reliably, and the emergency team often arrives without the one fact it needs most: when was the person last seen well?

Our solution

A browser app (no install) that walks anyone through BE-FAST in about a minute, measures the warning signs with the phone's camera and microphone, gives one clear instruction, and hands the hospital a structured report.

CheckHow it is measured
TTime"Last known well" recorded first; time-window guidance (≤4.5 h, ≤24 h).
BEBalance, EyesPose tracking: sway, lean, stepping. Iris tracking: both eyes follow a dot. Merged with the bystander's answer.
FFaceMediaPipe Face Landmarker (478 pts). Mouth-corner excursion from rest → smile in a face-aligned frame (cancels head tilt and distance); turned-head frames rejected. Asymmetry index + droop.
AArmsMediaPipe Pose: wrist height vs shoulder line over a 10 s eyes-closed hold — a camera pronator-drift test. Detects one-sided drift or failure to raise; ignores both arms tiring.
SSpeechCincinnati sentence: word error rate + on-device pause analysis + a trained slurred-speech model (TORGO) + bystander judgement (human in the loop).

Key features

  • One clear answer: any single warning sign → "Call 108 now" (paramedic rule).
  • Explainable: every finding shows its measurement, e.g. "left side moved 52% less".
  • ER handoff QR code: clinician report with findings, measurements and onset time.
  • Nearby hospitals (OpenStreetMap) with call and directions.
  • English · हिन्दी · தமிழ் results, read aloud for low-literacy users.
  • Offline-capable PWA for low-connectivity areas.

Safety, privacy, ethics

  • No video leaves the device — vision models run in the browser (WebAssembly).
  • Zero server storage: the report lives in the link's #fragment, which browsers never send; decoded input is validated.
  • Never a false all-clear: skipped/failed checks → "inconclusive".
  • No generated medical advice: fixed, reviewable templates instead of an LLM.
  • Framed as a screening aid, not a diagnosis.

Architecture

Camera/Mic → MediaPipe (WASM, GPU→CPU) + Web Audio + Web Speech → feature extractors (asymmetry, drift, WER, pauses) → pure-TypeScript rule engine → risk tier → i18n results · QR report (URL fragment) · hospitals (Overpass) Stack: Next.js 16 · React 19 · TypeScript · Tailwind 4 · MediaPipe Tasks · Vitest · Playwright · Service Worker

Impact & next steps

India has roughly 1.2 million new strokes a year (GBD 2021: 88 per 100,000; Behera et al., Sci Rep 2024), and limited specialist access outside cities. A free, private, offline-capable tool on the phones people already own can shorten the time to calling an ambulance and give hospitals a head start.

Next: calibrate thresholds on clinical data (Toronto NeuroFace, TORGO) and an ED pilot; add gait tests; connect to 108 dispatch; more Indian languages.
StrokeSense is a screening aid and does not provide a diagnosis. Thresholds are provisional and not clinically validated. If you suspect a stroke, call emergency services immediately (India: 108 / 112).
